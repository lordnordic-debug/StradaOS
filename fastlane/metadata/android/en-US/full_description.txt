<p><b>StradaOS</b> gives your car's Android head unit a new life: it replaces the generic factory menu with a modern home screen, without flashing anything or touching the system.</p>
<p>It is designed for head units that claim "Android 6+" but actually run Android 4.x, where the Play Store no longer works and most apps won't install. It also works on modern units.</p>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Live and offline map: download your country's map from the app and use it without internet.</li>
<li>Android Auto and CarPlay buttons: opens your unit's projection app or Open Headunit.</li>
<li>Your music on the home screen: title, artist and time of what plays over Bluetooth; with Android Auto, the album art too.</li>
<li>GPS speedometer, weather, clock, oil change reminder and a photo of your vehicle with its background removed.</li>
<li>Design your home screen: player on the left or right, normal, compact or side button bar, and foldable Android Auto and CarPlay buttons.</li>
<li>A bottom bar your way and hideable apps. The reverse camera keeps working.</li>
<li>Dark, light, scheduled or system theme, 10 colors and 9 languages.</li>
<li>Update notices with a QR code.</li>
</ul>
<p>Works on Android 4.1+. Under 1 MB. Free, no ads, open source (GPL-3.0).</p>
